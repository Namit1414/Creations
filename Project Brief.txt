# Project: Akarshan Creations | Premium Handmade Art & Personalized Gifting Website

This is a real business website, not a generic template, a wireframe, or a landing-page mockup. The website must be visually distinctive, responsive, easy to navigate, and designed to convert visitors into WhatsApp orders.

Create a website that helps customers:

1. Discover handmade products through beautiful photography.
2. Explore products by category and occasion.
3. Understand the customization possibilities.
4. View clearly labeled sample prices while the client finalizes pricing.
5. Send a WhatsApp enquiry for a particular product.

## 3. Brand identity and visual direction

Create an original visual identity specifically for Akarshan Creations.

### Visual concept

The design should combine:

* The warmth of handmade craftsmanship.
* The beauty of Indian art and traditional motifs.
* The charm of personalized gifting.
* The editorial elegance of a boutique creative studio.
* A modern, premium online shopping experience.

Use a refined palette consisting of:

* Warm ivory or cream as the primary background.
* Muted blush pink for selected accents.
* Terracotta or warm clay for emphasis.
* Deep plum or burgundy for strong contrast.
* Restrained champagne-gold details.
* Dark charcoal for readable body text.

Keep the background mostly light. Use color intentionally rather than filling every section with pink.

Typography should pair an elegant editorial serif for prominent headings with a clean, highly legible sans-serif for body text, navigation, prices, buttons, and product details.

Use generous whitespace, balanced layouts, subtle borders, restrained shadows, tasteful decorative details, and sophisticated image compositions.

Avoid:

* Generic AI-generated ecommerce templates.
* Excessive gradients, glassmorphism, or glowing effects.
* Overly childish typography and cartoon illustrations.
* Excessive rounded cards everywhere.
* Unnecessary animations that make the site feel slow.
* Fake luxury claims or visually extravagant elements that compete with the products.
* A repetitive grid of identical white cards throughout every page.

### Logo creation

Generate an original logo for Akarshan Creations.

The logo should be elegant, recognizable, and appropriate for a handmade art and gifting boutique. Explore a custom monogram or a subtle motif inspired by a flower, handcrafted ornament, creative flourish, or gift ribbon.

Create:

* A primary logo with the brand name.
* A compact icon or monogram suitable for a favicon and mobile navigation.
* A version that works on light and dark backgrounds.

Prefer a clean vector-style treatment that can be exported as SVG or recreated in SVG. Keep the mark legible at small sizes.

Do not use a premade unrelated brand logo or copy another company's identity.
